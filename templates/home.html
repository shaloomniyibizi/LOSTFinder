{# ---------- How it works ---------- #}
Safe & verified

How LOSTFinder Works

Designed to prevent false claims and protect personal items through official university protocol.

1 Post Instant Notice

Upload a description, the approximate campus location and date, and an optional photo of the item you lost or found.

Contact details are never shown publicly

2 Automated Match & Alert

Our matching engine compares category, keywords, location, and dates between lost and found reports and scores each possible match.

Matches are reviewed before contact

3 Safe Retrieval

Submit a claim with proof of ownership. Once security approves it, collect your item in person and sign the handover record.

Physical ID check required
{# ---------- Protocol banner ---------- #}
Official Protocol · Campus Security Office

High-Value Valuables Daily Escort Protocol

In accordance with university safety bylaws, all high-value electronics (laptops, tablets, smartphones), jewelry, and passports found across campus are gathered by cleaning and security staff and escorted to the Campus Security Office safe at the end of each day.
